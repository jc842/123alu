## The advantages of aluminum joinery

### Aesthetics and contemporary design

Aluminum joinery is available in a wide variety of **colors** and styles, which allows them to be adapted to all types of architecture. ### Durability and resistance

With their stainless properties, aluminum joinery does not rust, which makes it particularly resistant to bad weather. They retain their original appearance for many years, even under the effect of UV rays from the sun. Good maintenance ensures their longevity, and you will thus avoid frequent replacements.

### Thermal and acoustic insulation

Modern aluminum windows are designed to provide a **excellent level of insulation**. Thanks to thermal break systems, they reduce heat loss and promote optimal comfort inside your home. This also translates into savings on your energy bills, with a potential reduction of 15%.

Another of the many benefits of aluminum joinery lies in their **ease of maintenance**. A simple wipe with a sponge and regular cleaning is enough to maintain their shine and functionality. Unlike other materials, you will not need to repaint your frames every year, which saves time and money.
