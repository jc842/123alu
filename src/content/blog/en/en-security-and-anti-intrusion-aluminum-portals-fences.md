# Security & Automation: Aluminum Entry Gates and Perimeter Fencing

An aluminum perimeter gate is the primary physical defense and aesthetic signature of a modern private residence.

---

## 1. Sliding vs Swing Gate Mechanics
- **Sliding Gates (Coulissant)**: Ideal for sloped driveways or properties with limited courtyard depth.
- **Dual-Swing Gates (Battant)**: Classic aesthetic for spacious driveways, compatible with underground concealed motors.

## 2. Integrated Access Control & Smart Intercoms
Modern aluminum gate pillars seamlessly house IP video intercoms, biometric keypad entry, and smartphone remote unlocking via Wi-Fi.

## 3. Zero Maintenance & High Wind Resistance
Engineered aluminum slat profiles resist heavy wind shear without bending, requiring nothing more than an occasional freshwater rinse.
