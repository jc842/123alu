# Aluminum vs PVC Joinery: Comparing Durability, Aesthetics & Costs

Choosing between aluminum and PVC joinery involves balancing architectural requirements, aesthetic preferences, and renovation budgets.

---

## 1. Aesthetic Comparison & Glass Surface Area
- **Aluminum**: Ultra-slim frames allow up to 15% more natural sunlight than comparable PVC sections.
- **Color Finishes**: Virtually unlimited RAL powder-coat colors, textured architectural hues, and dual-color configurations (different shades inside and outside).

## 2. Structural Strength & Oversized Openings
Aluminum's high modulus of elasticity makes it the only viable material for wide-span floor-to-ceiling facades, curtain walls, and heavy acoustic glass units.

## 3. Sustainability & 100% Recyclability
Aluminum is infinitely recyclable without losing its mechanical properties. Over 75% of all aluminum ever produced globally remains in productive use today.
