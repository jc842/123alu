# Modern Aluminum Verandas: Architectural Home Extension Guide

Adding an aluminum veranda creates a four-season living area bathed in light, whether utilized as a sunroom, dining pavilion, or home office.

---

## 1. High-Performance Insulated Roof Panels
Modern veranda roofs combine double-glazed solar control glass skylights with high-density polyurethane sandwich panels (up to 85 mm thickness) to prevent summer overheating.

## 2. Solar Control Glass (4 Seasons)
Reflects up to 70% of infrared solar heat during summer while preserving interior warmth during winter cold snaps.

## 3. Seamless Foundation & Drainage Integration
Thermally broken base sills, embedded water runoffs, and insulated slab connections prevent cold bridges and interior floor moisture.
